We welcome new NHS patients who live within our catchment area. Call [0161&nbsp;445&nbsp;1957](tel:01614451957) if you have questions about your eligibility.

## Register online

[Register with Didsbury Medical Centre using the NHS registration service](https://gp-registration.nhs.uk/P84678/gpregistration/landing).

If you cannot register online, come to reception during opening hours for a paper form.

It is helpful to have your NHS number when registering. [Find your NHS number](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/online-services/find-nhs-number/).
