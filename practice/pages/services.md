Our doctors, nurses and wider team provide a range of services. Call [0161&nbsp;445&nbsp;1957](tel:01614451957) or ask at reception to arrange a clinic appointment.

## Clinics at the surgery

- Cervical screening
- Childhood and adult immunisations
- Asthma and COPD clinics
- Diabetes clinics
- NHS Health Checks
- Family planning and contraception
- Minor surgery, following referral by a practice doctor
- Travel advice and vaccinations, with charges for some services
- Maternity services alongside hospital antenatal care
