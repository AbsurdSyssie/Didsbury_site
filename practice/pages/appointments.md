## Request a GP appointment

[Use Accurx to request an appointment](https://florey.accurx.com/p/P84678).

- **Same-day urgent requests:** 8am to 9:30am on working days, for medically urgent problems.
- **Routine requests:** from 9:30am. These may take up to 24 hours to review. Do not submit an urgent request on the routine form.

Include clear information about what you need help with. The team will use this to arrange the most appropriate care. You may be directed to another local service.

If you need more urgent help, call [0161&nbsp;445&nbsp;1957](tel:01614451957). When the practice is closed, use [NHS 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/) or call [111](tel:111).

## If you cannot use the internet

Call our care navigators on [0161&nbsp;445&nbsp;1957](tel:01614451957) from 8am. They can complete the request on your behalf. Phone appointment requests are taken until appointments are full.

We aim to support continuity of care. You can ask for your named GP or a particular clinician, although this may not always be possible.

## Admin requests

Use the **admin or routine care request** option in [Accurx](https://florey.accurx.com/p/P84678) for prescriptions, sick notes, referral questions and test requests. The admin service is open 8am to 6:30pm on working days. Do not use the admin option to request a GP appointment.

## Nurse appointments

Call reception for help arranging a nurse appointment, including GP-requested blood tests, cervical screening, injections, travel vaccinations and long-term condition reviews.

## Evening and weekend appointments

Ask reception about Manchester Extended Access appointments. Your appointment may be at another surgery.

## Cancel an appointment

If you cannot attend, call reception so your appointment can be offered to someone else.
