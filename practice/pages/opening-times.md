## Evening and weekend appointments

Ask reception about GP, nurse and healthcare assistant appointments through Manchester Extended Access. These may be at a different surgery and are separate from our reception opening hours.

## When we are closed

For urgent medical help when the practice is closed, use [NHS 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/) or call [111](tel:111). In a life-threatening emergency, call [999](tel:999).
