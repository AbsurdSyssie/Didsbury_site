The Didsbury Medical Centre is an NHS GP surgery at 1 The Avenue, Didsbury, Manchester, M20 2ER.

## Patient Participation Group

Our Patient Participation Group brings patients, carers and surgery staff together to discuss patient experience and improve services. All registered patients can join. Ask at reception about taking part.

[More about the patient group](/patient-group/).
