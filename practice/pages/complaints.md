title: Complaints and feedback
---

We aim to provide the best possible service. If something has gone wrong, speak to the person concerned or ask reception for help.

## Make a complaint

Address your complaint to the practice manager. You can hand it in at reception or write to:

The Didsbury Medical Centre<br>
1 The Avenue<br>
Didsbury<br>
Manchester<br>
M20 2ER

Call [0161&nbsp;445&nbsp;1957](tel:01614451957) if you need help making a complaint.

## Complaining for someone else

We need the patient's written permission to discuss their care with another person. Ask reception for a complaints form and help with the consent process.

[Read about NHS complaints](https://www.nhs.uk/using-the-nhs/about-the-nhs/how-to-complain-to-the-nhs/).
