## Order a repeat prescription

You can order online using the [NHS App](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-app/).

You can also [request a repeat prescription through Accurx](https://florey.accurx.com/p/P84678/patient-form). Choose the **admin query** option, then repeat prescriptions, and list the medicines you need.

### In person or by post

Fill in your prescription slip and drop it into the surgery at 1 The Avenue, Didsbury, Manchester, M20 2ER.

You can also send your request by post. Include a stamped addressed envelope if you want your prescription returned by Royal Mail.

## Collecting your prescription

Repeat prescriptions are ready to collect after **2 working days**.

Choose a pharmacy to collect your prescription from. You can change your nominated pharmacy through the app or website where you order, at a participating pharmacy or at the surgery.

## Questions about your prescription
