title: Sick notes
---

[Request a sick note through Accurx](https://florey.accurx.com/p/P84678), using the admin request option.

You can also call [0161&nbsp;445&nbsp;1957](tel:01614451957) or visit reception. A GP will assess your request and may need more information or an appointment before issuing a note.

[Read NHS information about fit notes and self-certification](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/gps/getting-a-fit-note/).
