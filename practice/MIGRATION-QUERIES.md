# Questions before any official launch

These are editorial/governance questions, not patient-facing qualifications. User approval covers a personal demo only. No actual practice sign-off is claimed. Each question supplies source context and the exact requested answer.

1. **Whole-site approval and ownership.** The [skill sign-off checklist](../skill/gp-practice-website/references/sign-off-checklist.md) requires the practice to check every page. Please provide the approving person's name/role, explicit approval of phone, address, all hours, supplier routes, team, services and policy wording, and the genuine date they completed that check. Who will own practice-controlled repository/hosting accounts and recovery access? Do not enter the research/build date as `content_last_reviewed`.
2. **GP earnings year and cohort.** [Source earnings page](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/surgery-information/policies/gp-earnings/), capture 20260411052242, says £70,203, 2 full-time and 13 part-time GPs, “last financial year” without a year. What exact financial year does this declaration cover, and what is the current approved declaration, including whether a locum count is available? The source does not state a locum count, so it remains unknown and is not represented as zero. No inference of earnings year or cohort is allowed.
3. **Appointments and supplier capacity.** [Booking page](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/appointments/how-to-make-an-appointment/) indexed text and [live Accurx](https://florey.accurx.com/p/P84678) agree on urgent 8am–9:30am and routine from 9:30am/up-to-24-hour review, but overall online availability labels show 8am–6:30pm. Are these the current working-day windows, what should patients do when urgent capacity is exhausted, and what is the approved nurse booking route? The demo avoids response/booking guarantees and directs urgent needs to phone/111, not routine requests.
4. **Reception versus telephone hours.** [NHS hours](https://www.nhs.uk/services/gp-surgery/didsbury-medical-centre-dr-whitaker/P84678/contact-details-and-opening-times) and archive opening page agree on 8am–6:30pm weekdays. NHS says last confirmed 29 November 2024. Are phone line hours identical, and are there separate new-site/extended-access arrangements and planned future closures? Provide the full seven-day reception and phone schedule and dated exceptions. No old bank holidays or training closures were carried forward.
5. **Prescriptions and results.** [Repeat prescription page](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/prescriptions/how-to-request-a-repeat-prescription/) supports NHS App, exact Accurx route, slip and post; [prescriptions](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/prescriptions/) says two working days and pharmacist after 10am option 3. [Results](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/services/test-results/) says no routine result calls. Are all these methods/times still current? Provide approved wording, including any phone-order exception and results follow-up policy, without a generic promise that patients will be called.
6. **New-site location/access.** [NHS profile](https://www.nhs.uk/services/gp-surgery/didsbury-medical-centre-dr-whitaker/P84678) and [current CQC](https://www.cqc.org.uk/location/1-29701373279) confirm 1 The Avenue, M20 2ER. Old source map points at prior premises and some travel copy appears unchanged. Provide the correct map pin, parking/accessibility facilities, public transport and communication support for the new building. Demo uses an address search, not the stale pin, and makes no unsupported facility promises.
7. **CQC relocation.** [Provider services](https://www.cqc.org.uk/provider/1-199785118/services) archives 1-571125789 and lists 1-29701373279 as not yet inspected. Confirm the current location ID and approved CQC presentation. Do not transfer the old Good rating.
8. **Privacy and complaints.** [Privacy index](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/surgery-information/policies/privacy-notices/), captured 20231130194414, links old PDFs rather than an approved current notice. Please supply the current full patient/website notices, DPO route, approved complaints procedure and responsible contact. Until then the permitted notice-on-request holding page remains. No invented legal controller, retention or response-time claims.
9. **Staff, services, catchment and group.** [Doctors](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/surgery-information/team/doctors/), [nursing](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/surgery-information/team/nursing-team/), [practice team](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/surgery-information/team/practice-team/), [clinics](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/services/surgery-clinics/), [catchment](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/new-patients/catchment-area/) and [PPG](https://www.didsburymedicalcentre.co.uk/surgery-information/patient-group/) are dated source evidence. Provide the current approved staff names/roles and consent, actual clinic list, boundary and group joining details. The catchment map/form and old screening instructions were not copied.
10. **Accessibility, rights and operational review.** Please arrange an independent accessibility/safety review and confirm rights to practice naming/content before official use. The new statement is practical access help, not an unsupported WCAG compliance certification. Personal demo hosting is not the required practice-owned production arrangement. Check actual host behaviour, including true missing-path 404 responses, before any official release.
